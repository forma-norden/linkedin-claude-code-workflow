# linkedin-ad-creative-copy

Use this skill to select ad formats, write ad copy, and manage creative
refresh cycles for LinkedIn Ads.

## Ad Format Selection

| Format | Best For | Relative Cost | Reach |
|--------|----------|---------------|-------|
| Single Image | Standard prospecting, lead gen | Medium | High |
| Carousel | Multi-point storytelling, product features | Medium | High |
| Video | Brand awareness, complex concepts | High | Very High |
| Document (PDF) | In-feed gated content, frameworks | Low | High |
| Text Ad | Always-on retargeting | Very Low | Medium |
| Thought Leader Ad | Boosting organic posts from team | Medium | Very High |
| Conversation Ad | Direct response, event registration | High | Low (inbox) |

## Thought Leader Ads (Priority Format)

Promote organic posts from team members as sponsored content.
Benefits: 2-3x higher engagement than brand page ads, feels native, builds personal brand.

Setup:
1. Team member publishes organic post
2. Company page requests to promote the post
3. Team member approves
4. Ad runs as sponsored content from their profile

Best for: founder-led brands, practitioner marketing, B2B where trust matters.

## Copy Frameworks for Ads

### Problem-Solution (Lead Gen)
Line 1: Name the pain (reference their role)
Line 2: What happens if unsolved
Line 3: What the solution enables
CTA: Download / Get the guide / See how

### Social Proof (Retargeting)
Line 1: "[Company] achieved [result]"
Line 2: One sentence on how
CTA: See the case study / Read the breakdown

### Question Hook (Awareness)
Line 1: Ask a question they cannot ignore
Line 2: Tease the answer
CTA: Learn more

## Creative Refresh Cadence

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| CTR drops 30%+ from baseline | Refresh creative |
| Frequency exceeds 6.0 | Refresh or expand audience |
| 4-6 weeks elapsed | Scheduled refresh regardless |
| Engagement rate drops 50%+ | Urgent refresh needed |

## Creative Testing Framework

Always test:
1. **Image vs. carousel vs. video** (format test)
2. **Pain-led vs. outcome-led headline** (message test)
3. **CTA variant** (low friction vs. high intent)

Run tests with: minimum $500/variant, minimum 2 weeks, single variable per test.

## Execution Sequence

1. Select format based on campaign objective and funnel stage.
2. Write 3 copy variants using the frameworks above.
3. Select or create visual assets matching the format.
4. Launch with A/B test structure.
5. Monitor for creative fatigue signals.
6. Refresh on schedule or when signals trigger.

## Output Contract

Return:

- format recommendation with rationale
- 3 copy variants with headline, body, and CTA
- visual direction guidance
- testing plan with budget and timeline
- creative refresh schedule
